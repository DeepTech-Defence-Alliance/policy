# Technische Specificaties — Effects Tech Layer

## Comms Requirements

- **Data rate:** ≥ 10 Mbps (sensor data)
- **Latency:** ≤ 100ms (command & control)
- **Range:** ≥ 50 km (tactical), ≥ 500 km (LEO)
- **Availability:** ≥ 99% (redundant links)

## Sensor Requirements

- **EO:** ≥ 5cm GSD @ 1000m AGL
- **IR:** MWIR/LWIR, ≥ 10cm GSD
- **Radar:** SAR, ≥ 1m resolution
- **Update rate:** ≥ 1 Hz (real-time)

## Power Requirements

- **Energy density:** ≥ 250 Wh/kg (current), ≥ 400 Wh/kg (target)
- **Endurance:** ≥ 4 hours (tactical), ≥ 24 hours (persistent ISR)
- **Charging:** ≤ 1 hour (80%)
