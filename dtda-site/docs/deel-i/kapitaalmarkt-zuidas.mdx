# Kapitaalmarkt (Zuidas) — Financiering voor Dual-Use Tech

## Waarom Kapitaalmarkt?

**Traditional defense procurement financing:**
- Overheidsbudgetten (beperkt, langzame cycli)
- Export credit agencies (ITAR restricties)
- Private equity (exit onzekerheid)

**Problem:** Dual-use tech heeft **meer kapitaal** nodig dan overheidsopdrachten alleen kunnen financieren.

**Solution:** **Amsterdam Zuidas** als kapitaalmarkt hub voor dual-use defense tech.

---

**Waarom Amsterdam/Zuidas?**

| Factor | Amsterdam Advantage |

## Dual-Use Tech Investment Thesis

### What is Dual-Use?

**Dual-use** = Technology met **zowel civiele als militaire** toepassingen.

**Examples:**

| Technology | Civil Use | Military Use |
|------------|-----------|--------------|
| **EO/IR Sensors** | Agriculture, mapping | ISR, targeting |
| **Mesh Networking** | IoT, 5G private networks | Tactical comms, MANET |
| **Edge AI** | Autonomous vehicles, robotics | Drone autonomy, targeting |
| **Battery Tech** | EVs, grid storage | Drone endurance, ground vehicles |
| **Composite Materials** | Wind turbines, aerospace | Airframes, armor |

### Why Investors Care

**Traditional defense:** Opaque, long cycles, export restrictions.
**Dual-use:** Transparent, dual revenue streams, larger TAM.

**Investor advantages:**

1. **Dual Revenue Streams:**
   - Civiel: Commercial markets (agriculture, logistics, energy)
   - Militair: Government contracts (NATO, JEF, EU)

2. **Larger TAM (Total Addressable Market):**
   - Defense-only: €50B/jaar (EU defense spending)
   - Dual-use: €500B+ (automotive, energy, logistics, etc.)

3. **Less Regulatory Risk:**
   - EU dual-use framework (clearer than ITAR)
   - No vendor lock-in (open architectures)

4. **Strategic Importance:**
   - Governments support via capacity credits, availability fees
   - Economic security angle (not just defense)

## Financing Structures

### Capacity Credits + Capital Markets

**How it works:**

1. **Government** koopt capacity credits (€50M voor 1000 drones)
2. **Company** uses credits as **collateral** voor kapitaalmarkt financiering
3. **Investors** zien credits als **guaranteed future revenue**
4. **Company** investeert in surge tooling, supply chain

**Example:**
```
Company: TacticalDrone BV
Government contract: €50M capacity credits (1000 drones)

Capital raise (Zuidas):
  - IPO: €100M (use credits as revenue backlog)
  - Use of proceeds:
    - Manufacturing (€40M)
    - R&D (€30M)
    - Supply chain (€20M)
    - Working capital (€10M)

Investor thesis:
  - Guaranteed €50M military revenue (credits)
  - Dual-use: €200M+ civil market (logistics drones)
  - Total TAM: €250M+
```

### Availability Fees + Recurring Revenue

**Why investors like availability fees:**
- **Predictable cash flow** (annual payments)
- **SaaS-like model** (recurring revenue)
- **Lower risk** than project-based revenue

**Valuation multiples:**
- Traditional defense: 1-2x revenue (project-based)
- Dual-use + availability fees: 3-5x revenue (recurring)

✅ **Dual Revenue Validation:**

- Capacity credits vs. project-based

✅ **Export Control Compliance:**
- EU dual-use regulation alignment

✅ **Tech Sovereignty:**
- Open architectures (no vendor lock-in)
